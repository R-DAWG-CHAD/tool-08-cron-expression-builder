Understanding Crontab Syntax and Wildcards

Cron is a time-based job scheduler utility in Unix-like operating systems. System administrators and developers use crontab files to automate recurring maintenance tasks, database backups, email notifications, and data pipelines.

Crontab Field Order Structure:

* * * * * command_to_execute
│ │ │ │ └─── Day of Week (0 - 6) (Sunday=0)
│ │ │ └────── Month (1 - 12)
│ │ └───────── Day of Month (1 - 31)
│ └──────────── Hour (0 - 23)
└─────────────── Minute (0 - 59)
